# LaunchDesk Demo Company Handbook

LaunchDesk is a fictional customer support platform. All people and orders in this repository are invented.

## Shipping
Standard shipping is 3–5 business days after fulfillment. Express shipping is 1–2 business days. Tracking becomes available when a carrier scans a package. Shipping timelines are estimates, not guarantees.

## Returns
Unopened items can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery with an order reference. Final-sale items and digital goods are not eligible. Refunds go back to the original payment method after inspection and typically take 5–10 business days.

## Lost or delayed orders
If tracking has not updated for 7 calendar days, create a support ticket. Do not promise refunds or replacements before review.

## Warranty
Eligible hardware includes a 12-month manufacturing-defect warranty. Accidental damage and misuse are excluded.

## Escalation
Never request payment credentials, passwords, or full card numbers. Escalate suspected fraud, account compromise, legal requests, and threats immediately. The assistant can look up synthetic orders and create support tickets but may not issue refunds or change shipping addresses.
